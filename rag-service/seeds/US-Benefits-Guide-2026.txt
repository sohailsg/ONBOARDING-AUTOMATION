US Benefits Guide 2026. Health insurance covers 80 percent of premiums for employees, with dental and vision included. Enroll within 30 days of your start date.
--- page 2 ---
The 401k retirement plan matches 100 percent of contributions up to 5 percent of salary. Vesting is immediate. You may change contributions each pay period.
--- page 3 ---
Paid time off in the US is 20 days per year plus 10 federal holidays. New joiners accrue monthly. Unused PTO up to 10 days rolls over each January.
--- page 4 ---
Remote stipend in the US is USD 600 per year for home-office equipment. Submit receipts in the expenses tool. The stipend renews every January.
