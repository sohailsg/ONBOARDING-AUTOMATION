UK Employee Handbook 2026. Welcome to the company. Your probation period is 6 months from your start date. During probation you meet your manager biweekly for feedback and goal reviews.
--- page 2 ---
Remote home-office budget in the UK is GBP 500 per employee. Claim it via the expenses tool within your first 90 days. The budget covers a chair, desk, monitor and keyboard.
--- page 3 ---
Annual leave in the UK is 28 days per year including bank holidays. Book leave in the HR portal with at least 2 weeks notice for blocks over 5 days. Unused leave up to 5 days carries over to Q1.
--- page 4 ---
Sick pay in the UK follows Statutory Sick Pay (SSP) plus a company top-up at full pay for the first 10 working days per year. Notify your manager before 9am on the first day of absence.
