DE Leave Policy 2026. Annual leave in Germany is 30 days per calendar year for full-time employees. Submit requests in the HR portal at least 4 weeks ahead for summer months.
--- page 2 ---
Parental leave (Elternzeit) in Germany is up to 3 years per child with job protection. Elterngeld replaces 65 percent of net income for up to 14 months shared between parents.
--- page 3 ---
Sick leave in Germany is employer-paid at full salary for the first 6 weeks of illness. From week 7, health insurance pays Krankengeld. A doctor note is required from day 3.
--- page 4 ---
Home office allowance in Germany is EUR 400 per year. It covers internet, electricity and office supplies. Claim it with your tax documents each December.
